# BC public-college pathways toward applied AI/software/automation

Research checked 22 October 2026. Domestic Canadian citizen in BC, no completed high school, full-time employment, 10 study hours/week, must preserve family income. Official sources only. Prices CAD; current published estimates, not binding quotations. No applications submitted or institutions contacted.

## Decision

There is no verified, ready-to-enrol engineering pathway here that is both guaranteed evening/online and safely within a strict 10-hour total weekly budget. **Best next step: English/math assessment and targeted tuition-free upgrading, followed by a single carefully selected credit course.** Not finishing high school is not automatically disqualifying at Douglas or Langara, but mature admission does NOT waive English, mathematics, or course prerequisites.

- **Douglas CSIS Diploma:** best accessible formal diploma candidate to investigate first; explicit mature entry, part-time enrolment, programming/integration and machine-learning curriculum. Required-course scheduling remains unresolved; actual Fall 2026 introductory programming sections found are daytime.
- **BCIT ACIS → CSC → CST FLEX:** best documented evening/weekend, stackable technical route, but published workload exceeds 10 hours/week even for one COMP course. ACIS includes basic computing/Office, so use it only as the prescribed entry milestone, not as Douglas's career destination.
- **BCIT ASD:** useful software milestone, not a sufficient standalone replacement for a computing diploma for a new entrant.
- **Langara Computer Studies Diploma:** strong alternative with mature admission, scripting, software, databases, and transferable coursework. Math prerequisites and course scheduling need attention.
- **BCIT Artificial Intelligence Management:** potentially good later AI-adoption/business bridge; four prerequisite post-secondary courses make it NOT an immediate no-prerequisite entry route.
- **VCC Computer Systems Technology Diploma:** strong content fit, weak life fit; full-time in-person and explicit Grade 12 graduation/equivalent requirement.

## 1. BCIT Applied Computer Information Systems (ACIS), Associate Certificate, Part-time

Official program: https://www.bcit.ca/programs/applied-computer-information-systems-acis-associate-certificate-part-time-6992acert/

- **Admission:** English Studies 12 67% or approved Category 2 equivalent. Complete COMP 1002 before declaring. Page expects Grade 12-level English and math knowledge; individual prerequisites remain. PLAR is course-specific, through formal exams if available—not automatic credit for projects.
- **Delivery/time:** nights/weekends; online and blended delivery listed; individual sections determine attendance. Minimum 25 credits; “Typically completed in at least 5-7 terms due to prerequisites and scheduling.” Five-year completion limit.
- **Workload:** exact quote: “A 12-week COMP course requires 12-15 hours per week”; compressed six-week versions double workload. COMP 1516 itself says 14+ hours/week.
- **Costs:** exact quote: “The current total cost of this program is under $ 5,000 depending on electives and if eBooks are chosen.” Pay course-by-course; budget computer/internet separately.
- **Curriculum/value:** Windows concepts, Python fundamentals, HTML/CSS, Office; choose computing mathematics, SQL/database and communication electives corresponding to CSC. It is a formal BCIT associate certificate and a laddering foundation, not an AI-engineer qualification.
- **Funding:** BCIT's June 2026 eligible-program list includes “Applied Computer Information System | UHC8”; program page says financial assistance may be available. Confirm exact ACIS program-code mapping, course load and study-period approval with Financial Aid.
- **Blocker:** published weekly load exceeds 10 hours. For CSC progression, leave COMP 1516 until last, achieve 70%, then take COMP 2509 in the following term; do not assume self-taught tools satisfy prerequisites.

## 2. BCIT Computer Systems, Certificate, Part-time (CSC)

Official: https://www.bcit.ca/programs/computer-systems-certificate-part-time-5500cert/

- **Admission:** English Studies 12 67%/Category 2 equivalent; Grade 12-level English and math skills. COMP 1516 at 70% is required to declare. Crucial exact instruction: “Students only able to take 1 course per term must first complete ‘Applied Computer Information Systems (ACIS)’ Associate Certificate prior to starting the CSC, leaving COMP 1516 as the last course in ACIS.” Applicants taking two courses/term first complete COMP 1002 and COMM 1103 or recommended COMM 1106, then request a course plan.
- **Delivery:** blended; classes after 6 pm and weekends, Burnaby/Downtown/online sections. Not guaranteed entirely online.
- **Duration/workload:** 60 credits, typically 17 courses. Two years at three courses/term, three years at two; maximum five years. Published loads: 25+ hours/week for two courses and 40+ for three; one 12-week COMP course averages 12–15 hours/week. Program-abandonment rules also constrain extended pauses.
- **Costs:** “Students should budget $11,000 for tuition and books.” Course-by-course pricing; Windows 11-capable computer required.
- **Curriculum/value:** Python, Java, C, HTML/CSS, JavaScript, SQL, mathematics/discrete math, systems analysis, Agile, UX/UI and communication. Parallel to first year of full-time CST. Can progress to CST FLEX (another 60 credits; 120 total) and later an applied-computer-science degree, subject to admissions. This is a milestone, not already a diploma or degree. Do not add ACIS/ASD/CSC headline prices blindly: overlapping courses can count toward the ladder if selected correctly.
- **Funding:** “Computer Systems | UAM8” explicitly in BCIT eligible list. Students on funding timelines need a signed Course Planning Document before declaration. Aid approval and scheduling are not guaranteed.
- **Blocker:** incompatible with fixed 10 hours/week as published. Starting CSC immediately at one course/term also contradicts its ACIS-first instruction.

## 3. BCIT Applied Software Development (ASD), Associate Certificate, Part-time

Official: https://www.bcit.ca/programs/applied-software-development-asd-associate-certificate-part-time-6958acert/

- **Admission:** COMP 1002/equivalent PC-file-management knowledge; Grade 12 English/math or equivalent knowledge; formal declaration additionally lists English Studies 12 67%/Category 2 equivalent. Start COMP 1516 and earn 70% before declaration/progression. Course listing specifies 60% COMP 1002 or 50% ACIT 1420 for COMP 1516: obtain departmental approval for any knowledge-equivalency claim.
- **Delivery/duration:** blended, nights after 6 pm/weekends, January/April/September intakes; published “as little as 2 years with 1 course per term.”
- **Workload:** COMP 1516 14+ hours/week; COMP 2509 15+ hours/week including class/online/homework. Attendance/participation mandatory.
- **Cost:** approximately $4,000 depending on electives/eBooks; pay as you go.
- **Curriculum:** Python → object-oriented Java, business analysis/systems design; electives C/C++/C#, Agile Scrum, QA/testing.
- **Credential caveat:** BCIT explicitly says ASD “only functions as an entry-to-practice credential for those with other post-secondary IT education or related work experience”; later says the standalone credential works for those with other post-secondary Computing/IT education. Therefore don't market it to Douglas as a guaranteed standalone software job qualification.
- **Funding:** ASD is NOT named in BCIT's June 2026 eligible-program list; do not promise StudentAid. Ask whether approved courses can be funded within an eligible declared ladder program. This is not confirmation of ASD eligibility.
- **Blocker:** 10-hour cap; no proof yet of English/math/programming prerequisite readiness.

## 4. Douglas College Computing Studies and Information Systems (Diploma)

Official: https://www.douglascollege.ca/program/dpcsti

- **Entry:** general college admission PLUS Pre-calculus 11 C or Foundations of Math 11 C/equivalent. Mature general admission includes “Nineteen (19) years of age or older by the end of the first month of the first semester of attendance.” It is followed by **AND English Proficiency Requirement**.
  - General: https://www.douglascollege.ca/admissions/requirements-and-assessment-testing/general-college-admissions-requirements
  - English: https://www.douglascollege.ca/admissions/requirements-and-assessment-testing/english-language-proficiency-requirement
  - English routes include English Studies 12 C, Douglas College English Assessment, approved tests or ENGU completion/placement. ENGU 0355 Mastery is one route. Exact warning: “Students without this level of English-language proficiency are not admissible to College-level courses and programs but can register in Douglas College upgrading courses.”
  - CSIS 1175 accepts MATU 0410 C as an alternative to the stated Grade 11 math. Program-level equivalency still needs confirmation. Some later math/statistics courses require higher grades/Grade 12 math.
- **Delivery/duration:** New Westminster, explicitly full-time or part-time; Fall/Winter/Summer. 60 credits, nominal two years full-time. No whole-program online/evening guarantee or weekly total published in program material reviewed. At one 3-credit course every term, 20 courses/three terms per year is about 6.7 years before prerequisites, unavailable sections or breaks (calculated planning illustration, NOT an official completion promise).
- **Real schedule check:** https://www.douglascollege.ca/course/csis-1175 lists 15-week Introduction to Programming I. Fall 2026 sections displayed: Monday 11:30–14:20; Wednesday and Thursday 12:30–15:20, New Westminster. Thus “part-time” does not mean compatible with a daytime job. Winter availability must be checked separately. Homework time not stated.
- **Curriculum/value:** C#/.NET intro, advanced integrated development, databases, systems analysis/design, web JavaScript/AJAX, mobile apps, scripting for cybersecurity and required Fundamentals of Machine Learning in Data Science (CSIS 3290), plus business/communication and advanced electives. Formal public-college diploma and potential course-specific university transfer, not merely support training. Optional paid full-time co-op: minimum 420 hours, 9 extra credits, not necessarily compatible with retaining existing employment.
- **Current tuition:** Winter 2027 lower-level $121.40/credit; newer upper-level courses $145.40/credit. One 3-credit course tuition $364.20 or $436.20. Published total fee rows: $689.05 lower / $761.05 upper, before textbooks and individual adjustments. The lower PDF's extracted U-Pass cell is blank although its stated total includes more than visible component cells; confirm assessment, don't silently reconcile. A 60-credit base-tuition planning envelope at those two rates is $7,284–$8,724, not an institution program quotation; exact mix, recurring semester fees and future increases determine actual cost.
  - https://www.douglascollege.ca/fees-and-finances/tuition-and-fee-estimator/tuition-fee-charts
  - https://www.douglascollege.ca/sites/default/files/docs/fees-and-related-information/lower-level-course-fee-schedule-202710.pdf
  - https://www.douglascollege.ca/sites/default/files/docs/fees-and-related-information/upper-level-course-fee-schedule-202710.pdf
- **Aid:** Douglas explicitly allows potential part-time StudentAid for 3–8.5 credits/semester (20–59%), subject to residency, income, designated program and assessment. https://www.douglascollege.ca/fees-and-finances/student-loans-and-grants/loans-and-grants-process
- **Blockers:** math/English proof, daytime sections, no verified total-load fit. A degree is NOT required for this diploma; do not confuse it with Douglas post-degree/post-baccalaureate programs.

BSYS 4001 and 4005 show “Not offered this term” without current fees, so the complete program tuition could NOT be verified. Prerequisite courses cost extra; no required books/supplies per program page.

## Funding and practical first move

1. BCIT named-program eligibility evidence (June 2026, subject to change): https://www.bcit.ca/files/finaid/pdf/parttime_eligible.pdf . Includes ACIS-labelled entry, Computer Systems certificate and Computer Systems Technology diploma; ASD and Artificial Intelligence Management absent. Confirm exact declared program and course plan rather than inferring from overlapping coursework.
2. StudentAid part-time assistance: https://studentaidbc.ca/apply/part-time . Exact warning: “The part-time student loan program does not cover living costs.” Can support tuition, books, supplies, transport and eligible childcare; employment does not itself exclude, but household income/residency/program/load matter. Do not replace wages with an aid assumption.
3. BC Access Grant part-time: https://studentaidbc.ca/explore/grants-scholarships/bc-access-grant-part-time . Up to $1,000/year for qualifying low-/middle-income students at BC public institutions, eligible undergraduate certificate/diploma/degree; unclassified studies excluded. Automatically assessed with part-time aid, remaining financial need required. Current page family-of-four maximum-grant threshold $76,952; cutoff $106,295. Douglas's family size/income unknown.
4. StrongerBC future skills grant: https://www.workbc.ca/FutureSkillsGrant . Official page says applications open for 2026/27, up to $3,500 for eligible short-term training, BC residents 19+. This is NOT blanket coverage for every public-college course. None of the shortlisted credentials above was verified as a currently funded offering; check exact offering via EducationPlannerBC/provider before budgeting it.
5. Tuition-free readiness route: https://www.douglascollege.ca/programs-and-courses/academic-upgrading/english-upgrading states “ENGU and MATU courses are tuition free. Student fees still apply.” https://www.douglascollege.ca/programs-and-courses/academic-upgrading/math-upgrading confirms placement assessment and MATU 0410/0411 equivalence to relevant Grade 11 math. Do not conflate its six-week online MATU 0500 statistics refresher with zero-prerequisite upgrading: it already requires Grade 11 math C.
6. Adult Upgrading Grant: https://studentaidbc.ca/explore/grants-scholarships/adult-upgrading-grant-application . Current 2026/27 application; may cover fees/books/supplies/transport/unsubsidized childcare for eligible adults at approved public institutions. Individual financial eligibility requires assessment.
