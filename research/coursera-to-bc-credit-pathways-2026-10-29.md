# Coursera courses as credit toward a British Columbia credential

Research checked 29 October 2026 (PDT). The question is not whether Coursera advertises credit somewhere; it is whether a British Columbia college or university will apply the learning toward Douglas's BC credential.

## Direct answer

**No automatic or published BC transfer-credit agreement was found for the IBM AI Developer, IBM RAG and Agentic AI, or Google IT Automation with Python Professional Certificates.** The BC Transfer Guide and the reviewed official institution pages do not list a direct Coursera-to-BC articulation for these certificates.

The practical BC route is **Prior Learning Assessment and Recognition (PLAR)**: the Coursera certificate, verified identity/assessment record, ACE recommendation where available, course syllabi, labs, capstones, Git history, and an independent demonstration are evidence. A BC faculty assessor then decides whether Douglas's learning matches a specific course or program outcome. Credit is not awarded merely for possessing the Coursera certificate.

## Best target: Thompson Rivers University Open Learning

**Why it is the strongest fit:**

- TRU Open Learning's Bachelor of Computing Science explicitly says professional IT experience may be assessed through PLAR toward the degree.
- TRU's PLAR page lists industry training and certifications, continuing-studies courses, private study, workshops, and non-accredited institutions as learning that may be demonstrated and assessed for credit.
- Available pathways include a course-based portfolio, challenge examination, competency-based portfolio, and Credit Bank.
- The BCS Open Learning degree has a 15-TRU-credit residency requirement, although actual applicability of PLAR credit to required computing courses is decided by the program/assessor.
- TRU advising says private study and continuing-studies courses can be considered in an initial PLAR assessment and that PLAR can satisfy residency where program rules allow.

**Current blocker:** TRU's live PLAR page says new PLAR enrolments and assessment requests were temporarily paused effective July 2, with resumption anticipated in mid-to-late Fall 2026. That page must be checked and the PLAR team contacted before Douglas relies on this route.

## Strong alternatives

### University of the Fraser Valley — CIS Diploma / BCIS

- UFV PLAR explicitly covers independent study and courses ineligible for ordinary transfer credit.
- Assessment can be a course challenge or portfolio matched to course/program learning outcomes.
- The student must be admitted or eligible to register; only credit applicable to the intended program is assessed.
- A course challenge requires department approval and currently costs 75% of the course tuition. A pass or fail is recorded on the transcript.
- Best use: request assessment against a named COMP/CIS course only after comparing every learning outcome.

Sources:
- https://ufv.ca/plar/students-and-prospective-students/
- https://ufv.ca/plar/course-challenge-process/

### Kwantlen Polytechnic University — Computer Information Systems Diploma

- KPU PLAR recognizes formal learning that is ineligible for transfer credit plus informal/non-formal learning, work, and life experience.
- Assessment may use examinations, portfolios, demonstrations, interviews, worksite assessment, or industry-based evaluation.
- Credit must apply to the student's declared program and is awarded at department/program discretion.
- KPU currently charges the domestic course tuition for course credit or, for competency assessment, CAD $750 or the applicable domestic tuition amount, whichever is less.
- Best use: ask the CIS PLAR advisor whether the IBM/Google learning can be pre-assessed against specific INFO course outcomes.

Source:
- https://calendar.kpu.ca/admissions/recognition-prior-learning/

### Douglas College — Computing Studies and Information Systems Diploma

- Douglas offers PLAR to students admitted to a program when prior formal or informal learning matches course/program outcomes.
- Methods include challenge examination, workplace-based assessment, and portfolio-assisted assessment.
- Not every CSIS course is PLAR eligible, and availability depends on the department and a qualified assessor.
- Best use: ask the CSIS Program Chair which introductory programming, web, database, or software-development courses are currently open to PLAR before paying for a Coursera certificate for credit purposes.

Source:
- https://www.douglascollege.ca/admissions/requirements-and-assessment-testing/prior-learning-assessment-and-recognition

### BCIT Flexible Learning — Computing

- BCIT says PLAR exams currently exist for most computer-application courses, some database and mathematics courses, and many popular programming languages.
- Computing PLAR is a written exam, course by course. Not all courses are available; total PLAR credit is limited; fees are non-refundable.
- Best use: email `cstpts@bcit.ca` with the target credential and exact Coursera syllabus to ask which COMP course challenge exams exist.

Source:
- https://www.bcit.ca/computing-academic-studies/computing/part-time-studies/comp-requirements/

## Institutions not supported as direct Coursera-credit answers

- **UBC:** UBC's own Coursera pilot page explicitly says its Coursera offerings were non-degree and non-credit and could not substitute for UBC for-credit curriculum. A specific UBC graduate admissions prerequisite page accepts certain Coursera certificates as prerequisite evidence, but that is not academic credit toward a computing credential.
- **SFU:** no current official evidence was found that IBM/Google Coursera Professional Certificates receive academic credit. SFU Continuing Studies programs are generally non-credit.

## Required evidence packet

Before asking for PLAR, preserve:

1. Verified Coursera completion certificate and identity-verified records.
2. ACE transcript/recommendation for IBM AI Developer or Google IT Automation, when applicable.
3. Full course syllabus and module learning outcomes.
4. Assessment methods, scores, lab records, and time/effort evidence.
5. Capstone source code, Git history, tests, deployment notes, and project report.
6. A mapping from each Coursera outcome to one specific BC course outcome.
7. An independent explanation/debugging demonstration proving Douglas owns the learning.

## Safest next action

Ask **TRU Open Learning PLAR and the Bachelor of Computing Science advisor** for a written pre-assessment before enrolling in Coursera primarily for credit. The request should name the exact certificates and ask:

- whether each certificate is in TRU's Credit Bank or requires individual PLAR;
- which BCS courses or elective credits could potentially be assessed;
- whether the ACE recommendation will be considered as supporting evidence;
- what portfolio or challenge method would apply;
- current PLAR availability, fees, limits, and residency implications.

Do not promise Douglas a credit amount until the BC institution replies in writing. The likely route is assessed PLAR credit, not automatic transfer credit.
