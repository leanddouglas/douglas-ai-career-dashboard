## 1. Douglas College — Computing Studies and Information Systems Diploma

Official program: https://www.douglascollege.ca/program/dpcsti

- Public BC college; 60-credit diploma; full-time or part-time.
- Mature general admission includes applicants age 19 or older, but English proficiency and program mathematics requirements still apply.
- Pre-calculus 11 C or Foundations of Mathematics 11 C/equivalent is required. Douglas upgrading and assessment routes exist; MATU 0410 appears as a course prerequisite alternative, but program-level equivalency needs written confirmation.
- Curriculum includes programming, databases, systems analysis/design, web development, scripting, mobile applications, business/communication, and a required machine-learning course.
- Reviewed Fall 2026 introductory programming sections were daytime. “Part-time” does not prove compatibility with full-time daytime work.
- Best next proof: ask admissions/program advising whether MATU 0410 satisfies program entry, which upcoming CSIS 1175 sections are evening/online/hybrid, and the realistic weekly total for one course.

## 2. Thompson Rivers University Open Learning — Bachelor of Computing Science

Official program: https://tru.ca/distance/programs/technology/bachelor-of-computing-science.html

- Public BC university; Open Learning/online degree; 120 credits, including at least 45 upper-level credits.
- First-year entry lists Pre-calculus 12 or Foundations of Mathematics 12 at C+ and English Studies 12/English First Peoples 12 at 73%, or equivalents.
- Crucial official statement: applicants who have not met those requirements but are high-school graduates **or mature students** will be conditionally admitted to the Bachelor of Computing Science program.
- Curriculum includes programming, data structures/algorithms, software architecture/design, databases, software engineering, networks, security, human-computer interaction, Applied Artificial Intelligence, and a computing-science project.
- TRU identifies possible laddering from a computing diploma and possible Prior Learning Assessment and Recognition for professional IT experience. Neither is automatic credit for polished projects.
- Open Learning 2026/27 tuition varies by course. TRU lists an average of CAD $183.04 per credit for Canadian citizens/permanent residents in BC/Yukon, plus course administration and technology/material fees. Use the course cart for an actual estimate.
- Best next proof: request a program plan for a mature first-year entrant, identify conditional/upgrading courses, and confirm whether one course at a time fits the degree's sequencing and completion rules.

## 3. University of the Fraser Valley — Computer Information Systems Diploma / BCIS

Official degree and entry rules: https://www.ufv.ca/calendar/current/ProgramsC-E/CIS_BCIS.htm

Official diploma overview: https://www.ufv.ca/computing/programs/cis-diploma/

- Public BC university. The CIS certificate, 60-credit diploma, and approximately 122-credit Bachelor of Computer Information Systems form a ladder.
- Entry accepts one of: BC secondary graduation/equivalent, mature student age 19+, or at least nine university-level credits with CGPA 2.0.
- Mature status does not remove mathematics or English requirements. Current accepted mathematics routes include specified Grade 12 math or UFV upgrading courses such as MATH 092/093, MATH 096, or MATH 110 at required grades.
- Curriculum includes web publishing, hardware, programming, networking, security, analysis/design, databases, data structures/algorithms, communications, and business. The degree adds software development, systems/networking or security concentration options.
- UFV states that all courses are offered in Abbotsford; some first-year CIS courses are offered in Chilliwack and online, and some may be offered in the evening for working part-time students.
- That wording does not guarantee a complete evening/online diploma. Admittance also does not guarantee a particular section.
- Best next proof: obtain a one-course-per-term map showing actual evening/online sections, prerequisites, and total weekly work before treating UFV as job-compatible.

## 4. Kwantlen Polytechnic University — Computer Information Systems Diploma

Official program: https://calendar.kpu.ca/programs-az/business/computer-information-systems/computer-information-systems-diploma/

Program overview: https://www.kpu.ca/melville/computer-information-systems-diploma

Admissions: https://calendar.kpu.ca/admissions

- Public BC polytechnic university; 60-credit Diploma in Computer Information Systems.
- KPU's mature applicant category includes applicants age 19+ on the first day of the term. Undergraduate English proficiency still applies.
- Program admission requires mathematics at KPU Level E1. KPU's overview identifies Foundations of Mathematics 11 C+, an equivalent MATQ course, or an equivalent Math Placement Test result.
- Curriculum includes programming, systems analysis/design, operating systems, networking, web applications, discrete mathematics, databases, data structures, security, communications, and a system-development project.
- The diploma can ladder into KPU's Bachelor of Technology in Information Technology. Co-op is optional and has additional requirements.
- Current official program pages reviewed do not establish that the full diploma can be completed through evening/online sections within ten hours/week.
- Best next proof: ask advising for a one-course-per-term plan, actual Surrey/online/evening sections, maximum completion time, and complete domestic cost.

## 5. Langara College — Computer Studies Diploma

Official program: https://langara.ca/programs-courses/computer-studies-diploma

- Public BC college; 60-credit diploma; full-time or part-time; January, May, and September starts.
- Domestic admission accepts BC Grade 12/equivalent **or age 19+**, plus the specified English category.
- Course admission has mathematics traps: required programming courses use Math Diagnostic Test or Grade 12/course alternatives, and some prerequisites are valid only three years.
- Curriculum includes programming, object-oriented design, algorithms/data structures, Unix/scripting, databases, web development, systems analysis, networking, and communication.
- Official domestic minimum-program tuition estimate in the earlier research snapshot was CAD $6,586, with additional costs and fees; verify the current figure before budgeting.
- No complete evening/online route or total weekly workload has been verified.
- Best next proof: request an actual one-course-per-term plan with section times, delivery mode, prerequisite route, homework estimate, and maximum completion period.

## Strong technical alternative: BCIT Flexible Learning

- BCIT ACIS/Computer Systems/CST FLEX has the clearest published evening/weekend ladder among the reviewed institutions.
- It remains outside the corrected top five because BCIT says a 12-week COMP course requires approximately 12–15 hours/week, already above Douglas's ten-hour total cap.
- Reconsider only if Douglas can safely increase weekly capacity or BCIT provides a documented lower-load route.

## Required decision gate

Before enrollment, each candidate must pass all four gates:

1. **Admission:** documented mature/alternative entry plus English/math prerequisites.
2. **Schedule:** actual required sections and all homework fit eight planned hours plus two contingency hours.
3. **Affordability:** complete gross cost is manageable before relying on unapproved aid.
4. **Credential relevance:** assessed programming, APIs/data, testing, security, software design, teamwork, and portfolio evidence support the target role.

Unknown facts remain **unresolved**, not favorable assumptions. No institution was contacted and no admission, funding, transfer credit, or employment outcome is guaranteed.
