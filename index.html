Protected execution plan

Ten hours. One clear lane.

Eight committed study hours plus two contingency hours. Never enroll in overlapping full workloads or rely on unapproved aid.

Weekly capacity

8 + 2 rule

8 focused
2 buffer

The buffer absorbs difficult debugging, family interruptions and peak assignment weeks. A program whose normal published load exceeds ten hours does not fit, regardless of marketing flexibility.

Decision tree

Before any payment.

Admission

Pass: documented direct or achievable alternative entry.
Unresolved: get written clarification.
Fail: use targeted upgrading—not wishful equivalency.

Schedule

Pass: all class, homework, group and exam time fits 8+2.
Unresolved: request a term map.
Fail: reject or defer.

Funding

Base case: affordable before aid.
Confirmed grant: lowers burden.
Loan: financing, not a discount. Never treat eligibility as an approved award.
